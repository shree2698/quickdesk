# Laptop and Hardware Request Policy

## Standard Hardware Refresh
Employees are eligible for a laptop refresh every 3 years. The IT department will contact you when your device is nearing its refresh date. 

## Requesting New Hardware
If your laptop is broken, lost, or no longer sufficient for your job duties:
1. Submit an IT Support ticket with the category "Hardware Request".
2. Briefly explain why a new device or upgrade is necessary.
3. Attach any relevant error logs or photos if the device is damaged.

## Available Models
- **Standard**: 14-inch display, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD (For general use)
- **Developer**: 16-inch display, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD (Requires engineering manager approval)

Please allow 3-5 business days for IT to process the request and ship the hardware.
