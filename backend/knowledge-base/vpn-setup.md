# Corporate VPN Setup Guide

## Prerequisites
- Active corporate credentials
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) set up on your mobile device
- Approved internet connection

## Installation
1. Go to the Employee Portal and download the "QuickDesk VPN Client".
2. Run the installer and grant administrator permissions if prompted.
3. Open the VPN application from your system tray.

## Connection Instructions
1. Enter your corporate email address in the "Username" field.
2. Enter your corporate password.
3. When prompted, approve the MFA request on your mobile authenticator app.
4. The status icon will turn green to indicate a successful connection.

## Troubleshooting
- **Error "TLS Handshake Failed"**: Usually indicates your home router is blocking the required UDP ports. Try restarting your home router.
- **Connection Drops**: Ensure you are not switching networks while connected.
- If problems persist, please submit an IT support ticket.
