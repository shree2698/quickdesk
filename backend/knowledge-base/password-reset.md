# Password Reset Policy and Guide

## Password Requirements
- Minimum of 12 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character
- Cannot be one of your last 5 used passwords
- Must be reset every 90 days

## Self-Service Reset
If you have forgotten your password but still have access to your MFA device:
1. Navigate to `https://login.quickdesk.com/reset`
2. Enter your corporate email address.
3. Enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your authenticator app or backup phone number.
4. Follow the prompts to create a new password.

## Locked Accounts
If your account is locked due to too many failed login attempts:
- The lockout period is 15 minutes.
- You can wait for the lockout to expire and try again, or you can use the Self-Service Reset option.
- If you no longer have access to your MFA device, you must create an IT Support Ticket and verify your identity with a manager.
