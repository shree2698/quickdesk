# Password Reset Policy

## Overview

Employees can reset their company password using the Self-Service Password Portal.

---

## Requirements

Passwords must:

- Be at least 12 characters long
- Include uppercase letters
- Include lowercase letters
- Include numbers
- Include special characters

---

## Reset Process

1. Visit the Password Portal.
2. Login using your employee ID.
3. Verify your identity using MFA.
4. Enter a new password.
5. Confirm the password.
6. Submit.

---

## Locked Account

Accounts are automatically locked after five failed login attempts.

Wait 15 minutes or contact IT Support.

---

## Password Expiry

Passwords expire every 90 days.

Employees receive reminder emails beginning 7 days before expiration.

---

## Security Best Practices

- Never share passwords.
- Do not reuse previous passwords.
- Use a password manager whenever possible.
