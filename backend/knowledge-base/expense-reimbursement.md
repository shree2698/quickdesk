# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses
- Work-related travel (flights, hotels, transport)
- Client entertainment (within budget limits)
- Approved software licenses (e.g., IDEs, design tools)
- Home office equipment (up to $500 one-time allowance)

## Submission Process
1. Retain all itemized receipts. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient.
2. Log in to the Finance Portal and create a new "Expense Report".
3. Upload PDF or image scans of your receipts.
4. Assign the correct category to each expense.
5. Submit for managerial approval.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are paid out on the 15th and 30th of each month. 
Please allow up to 5 business days for Finance processing after your manager approves the expense. If you have an urgent request (e.g., software subscription blocking your work), please submit a ticket to the Finance queue.
