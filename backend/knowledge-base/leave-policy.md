# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Employees receive 24 paid leave days every calendar year.

---

## Casual Leave

Employees may take up to 12 casual leave days annually.

Approval from the reporting manager is required.

---

## Sick Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 sick leave days.

Medical certificates are required for leave exceeding two consecutive days.

---

## Applying for Leave

1. Login to the Employee Portal.
2. Navigate to Leave Management.
3. Select leave type.
4. Choose dates.
5. Submit the request.

---

## Approval Process

Employee

↓

Reporting Manager

↓

HR

↓

Approved

---

## Emergency Leave

Employees should notify both their manager and HR as soon as possible.

---

## Leave Cancellation

Leave requests may be cancelled before approval through the Employee Portal.
