# Leave Policy

## Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued monthly. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis.

## Requesting Leave
1. Log in to the HR Portal.
2. Navigate to "Time Off" > "Request Time Off".
3. Select your dates and the type of leave.
4. Leave requests of 3 days or less should be submitted at least one week in advance.
5. Leave requests of 4 or more days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Leave Balance Updates
Please note that leave balances in the HR Portal are typically updated after the payroll cycle closes. Leave requests that are approved in the last 30 days might not reflect in your current balance until the next cycle.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note may be required if sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.
