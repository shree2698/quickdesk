# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses

Employees may claim reimbursement for:

- Business travel
- Hotel accommodation
- Client meals
- Internet expenses
- Office supplies

---

## Required Documents

Every claim must include:

- Original receipt
- Invoice
- Payment proof

---

## Submission Process

1. Login to the Expense Portal.
2. Select "New Claim".
3. Upload receipts.
4. Enter expense details.
5. Submit for approval.

---

## Approval Workflow

Employee

↓

Manager

↓

Finance

↓

Payment

---

## Processing Time

Approved reimbursements are processed within seven business days.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not eligible:

- Personal shopping
- Traffic fines
- Entertainment expenses
- Alcohol purchases
