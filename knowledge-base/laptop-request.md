# Laptop Request Process

## Overview

Employees may request a new laptop for:

- New joining
- Hardware failure
- Upgrade requirement
- Replacement of damaged devices

---

## Request Process

1. Create a Helpdesk Ticket.
2. Select category "IT".
3. Choose request type "Laptop Request".
4. Provide business justification.
5. Submit the request.

---

## Approval Flow

Employee

↓

Manager Approval

↓

IT Team

↓

Procurement

↓

Delivery

---

## Standard Delivery Time

Approved requests are fulfilled within five business days.

---

## Urgent Requests

Urgent laptop requests require manager approval with justification.

---

## Laptop Replacement

Replacement is allowed if:

- Device is older than four years
- Hardware failure cannot be repaired
- Business requirement changes

---

## Asset Return

Employees receiving replacement devices must return the previous laptop to the IT department.
