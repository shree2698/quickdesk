# VPN Setup Guide

## Overview

This guide explains how employees can install, configure, and troubleshoot the company VPN.

---

## Supported Operating Systems

- Windows 10/11
- macOS Ventura or later
- Ubuntu 22.04 LTS

---

## Installation Steps

### Windows

1. Download the VPN Client from the Internal Software Portal.
2. Install the application.
3. Restart your computer.
4. Launch the VPN Client.
5. Login using your company email and password.
6. Complete Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
7. Click **Connect**.

---

## Common Issues

### VPN Not Connecting

Possible causes:

- Internet connection unavailable
- Incorrect credentials
- Expired password
- Firewall blocking VPN
- VPN server maintenance

Try the following:

1. Verify your internet connection.
2. Restart your computer.
3. Restart the VPN application.
4. Re-enter your credentials.
5. Disable personal firewall temporarily.
6. Contact IT if the issue persists.

---

### Authentication Failed

Possible reasons:

- Wrong password
- MFA failure
- Locked account

Solution:

- Reset your password.
- Retry MFA.
- Contact IT Support.
